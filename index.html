About the Artist

Jesse King makes art out of necessity. Based in New England, his work is heavily influenced by years spent in the punk and skate scenes—places where being rough around the edges isn't just accepted, it's the point.

His drawings and oil paintings are fast, aggressive, and honest. He isn't interested in making things look pretty or polished. Instead, his work channels the harsh reality of living in a state of disillusionment—shedding light on the shambles of intoxicating and destructive love, the chaos and instability of a life on the edge, and the wrath of a party that never ended.

Returning to his art full-time, King aims to express his internal experience. He paints and draws because he has to get it out—his art gives a way for his turbulent existence to scream at the world. He is currently working out of his studio, doing board graphics, taking commissions, and selling original pieces.
